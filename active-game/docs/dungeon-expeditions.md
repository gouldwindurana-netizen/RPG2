# Dungeon expeditions

Each exterior dungeon entrance still leads to its original approach map. Stairs now continue into separate interiors. There are 33 dungeon floors across six expeditions, including 27 new maps.

| Expedition | Floors | Preparation focus |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Merewatch Bellworks | 3 | Brass frames, fragile support enemies, lightning, and a reserved healing charge |
| Impounding Tollhouse | 4 | Armored escorts, disruptive casters, remedies, and lodging money |
| Flooded Granary | 5 | Poisonous swarms, antitoxins, group attacks, and sound supply branches |
| Orchard Highroad | 6 | Root ambushes, lingering conditions, cross-locks, and protected seed stores |
| Crown Road Handover | 7 | Armored guardians, support enemies, clear tonic, and receiving-ward records |
| Last Service Passage | 8 | Three separated guardians, ordered cross-locks, emergency reserves, and staged resupply |

The extra floors deepen the existing account of maintenance crews, confiscation, spoiled stores, contamination, prisoner release, and the evacuation. Twenty-seven readable records add evidence and party responses without restoring permanent losses or changing established endings. Old saves already past a moved guardian retain their completed story.

## Route decisions

Supply caches occupy optional branches. They contain modest consumables and stay empty after collection. Reading a record or using stairs never restores HP or spell charges.

From floor three onward, a wheel can latch open a service lift between that floor and the approach. Both directions persist through retreat and reload. Floor four and five routes use a cross-lock; floors six and higher add a second lock whose wheel is beyond the first. The stairs behind the party remain reachable before opening either lock. The final route also retains its intermediate guardian conditions.

Area maps show the current floor, ordinary up/down stairs, `⇄` lifts, caches, and landmarks. The World Map still locates the expedition at one exterior destination. It does not add a second entrance for each interior floor.

## Prepare, attempt, regroup

Map and Journal preparation notes are available in nearby towns as well as inside the dungeon. They show actual medicine, HP, conditions, and remaining charges. Suggested quantities are advice, not entry requirements. Shops do not buy anything automatically.

Later floors unlock larger fixed regional groups. They do not increase monster stats to follow the party's level. Repeated normal battles remain available for training and supplies. Existing inns, shelters, and discovered lifts allow a party to return, recover, buy a useful spell or equipment upgrade, and attempt a longer push.

Ordinary rewards are lower than in the preceding build: 50% EXP and 65% gold. Boss and rare budgets retain 65% EXP and 75% gold. Existing earned levels, EXP, gold, prices, and unlock thresholds are preserved. See `depth-economy.md` for the tested routes and their limits.
