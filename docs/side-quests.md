# Side quests — The Road Beyond the Bells

Companion to the [expanded campaign](story.md) and [combat brief](combat-design.md). These are campaign quest designs. SQ-R1 has a small playable version in the First Watch demo; the other quests remain to be implemented.

There are **12 optional quests**: four two-part companion stories and four regional stories. The main campaign always rescues Neri, provides a viable evacuation, accounts for the missing crew, shuts down the captive-fed bells, and brings Vale to a hearing. Optional quests change relationships, local arrangements, keepsakes, and epilogue scenes; skipping one does not secretly condemn anyone.

Rewards below are finite supplies, modest equipment alternatives, or presentation changes. They do not grant unlimited charges, resurrect memories, or unlock an indispensable combat command. Final item statistics belong to combat balancing.

| Chapter Four: Veyr, before the final engine assault | Finish accessible companion follow-ups at the allied refuge. | SQ-R4 — One More Performance. |

SQ-R1 alone belongs in the first 10–15-minute prototype. It stays within the occupied camp and can be completed during departure preparations. Later quests are full-campaign content. There are no hidden real-time deadlines: advancing to the final assault gives an explicit notice, and unfinished optional quests receive ordinary epilogue defaults rather than surprise deaths. Refuge-based NPCs relocate with the main evacuation instead of vanishing when a town is left behind.

## Mara's companion story

### SQ-M1 — A Signature in Wax

**Available:** Chapter Two, after Leth releases the main transfer records. **NPCs:** Leth and Dena, a former convoy passenger.

Leth finds Mara's signature on a separate convoy receipt. The woman named on it is alive in the Dunmere refuge and wants an explanation, but does not want her children hearing her history from a crowd.

1. Compare the receipt with a gate register to establish what Mara signed, without replacing the main campaign's evidence.
2. Ask Dena where she wants the conversation held, then let her question Mara. Mara describes what she saw and what she failed to ask.
3. Bring Dena's corrections back to Leth, including an error that placed another passenger's debt under her name.

**Resolution:** With Dena's agreement, enter her account either under her name or as a protected statement. Neither route suppresses the transport evidence. Mara cannot obtain forgiveness by choosing the right dialogue.

**Reward and later consequence:** Dena returns Mara's old convoy buckler, recovered among seized belongings. Paired with a shortblade, it is a defensive weapon-slot loadout that trades some attack for protection; it does not require a new offhand slot. At Vale's hearing, Dena either speaks publicly or submits her protected statement. Her epilogue acknowledges the record was corrected, without declaring her friendship with Mara.

### SQ-M2 — Orders for the Living

**Available:** Chapter Three after SQ-M1 and Halden's main-story decision to let refugees pass. **NPC:** Captain Halden.

Halden's volunteers can help the new shelters, but several residents recognize the uniforms. He asks Mara to settle the argument. She refuses to settle it without hearing the residents.

1. Visit a shelter meeting and hear two concrete concerns: guards controlling food queues and guards keeping lists without consent.
2. Inspect the old watchhouse with Halden, recovering blankets while fighting one optional encounter with Hush nesting beyond its failed ward.
3. Draft the volunteers' service terms with the residents present.

**Resolution:** Assign the willing volunteers to a civilian-supervised watch or have them surrender their insignia and join ordinary maintenance crews. Halden accepts either arrangement. His main-story aid and responsibility for his former orders remain established.

**Reward and later consequence:** Halden repairs Mara's traveling cloak and supplies a finite recovery kit. In Chapter Four, these particular volunteers appear either beside an elected watchkeeper or carrying stretchers without rank badges. Their duties change, not the evacuation's success. At camp, Mara finally asks someone else to check her armor straps.

## Tavin's companion story

### SQ-T1 — Two Spoons

**Available:** Chapter Two after Neri's mandatory rescue and after she asks for some space. **NPC:** Neri.

Neri invites Tavin to help cook for the refuge. He arrives with the ingredients for the stew she used to make. She has already planned something different.

1. Ask three residents what food they can eat, learning that an old family recipe will not suit the whole table.
2. Sort the existing pantry with Neri and choose a workable dish; ingredients come from the refuge's allotted meal supplies, not someone else's emergency rations.
3. Prepare and serve the meal through short interactions and a conversation, without a cooking minigame.

**Resolution:** Neri can invite a story about the old stew while they work, or the player can follow her interest in discussing the meal she is making now. If she declines a memory conversation, Tavin respects it. Neither version makes her remember.

**Reward and later consequence:** Neri packs a finite pair of restorative meals for the party. A new camp exchange has Tavin asking what someone likes before telling them what they used to like. In Chapter Four, the refuge menu names the dish they actually made together.

### SQ-T2 — The Letter I Didn't Send

**Available:** Chapter Three after SQ-T1. **NPCs:** Neri and a retired postmaster.

Neri receives an old delivery notice for letters she wrote before her imprisonment. She wants to decide what to do with them and asks Tavin to retrieve the sealed packet.

1. Search the abandoned posthouse along an optional daylight spur from a functioning shelter, matching delivery marks with the postmaster's surviving index.
2. Defeat the Hush occupying its unwarded sorting room and recover the packet alongside ordinary undelivered mail.
3. Return Neri's letters unopened. Read one together only if she explicitly invites Tavin to do so.

**Resolution:** Neri chooses whether to keep the packet privately or entrust it to Tavin until she feels ready. The player helps arrange that choice rather than using the letters as a test of her identity.

**Reward and later consequence:** The postmaster gives Tavin a weatherproof courier mantle, an equipment alternative. Neri's kitchen later has either a locked letter box or a shelf Tavin built for her. A new letter from Neri describes a day she has just lived; it is not a recovered memory.

## Iona's companion story

### SQ-I1 — Names in Their Own Hand

**Available:** Chapter Two at Dunmere's refuge hospice. **NPCs:** Iona's former patient Reed and the hospice registrar.

A leaking roof has ruined several personal notebooks. The registrar wants to rebuild them from transfer records. Reed wants his chosen name on the new cover, although the official record gives him another one.

1. Compare the damaged shelf labels with the dry storage index to return each surviving notebook to its owner.
2. Speak privately with three willing patients about which details they want copied. One wants a family address; another wants yesterday's sketch; Reed wants a blank opening page.
3. Help the registrar separate the confidential administrative record from each person's own book.

**Resolution:** Let Reed write his cover himself or write it at his request. His chosen name is honored in both cases. The dispute concerns who controls the book, not whether lost memories can be repaired.

**Reward and later consequence:** The registrar gives Iona a durable field satchel and a finite antidote supply. Refuge patients later carry visibly different notebooks rather than standardized identities. Reed appears in the epilogue drawing visitors as they are now, with the signature he chose.

### SQ-I2 — Enough for Tonight

**Available:** Chapter Three after SQ-I1, during the main campaign's training period. **NPCs:** Sen and Mell, an apprentice healer.

Mell keeps volunteering for extra practice because she fears being useless. Iona recognizes her own habit of treating exhaustion as proof of care. This is a supervised practice ward, not the sole protection around sleeping refugees.

1. Review Mell's practice sheet and supplies; discover that her accounting and patient notes are meticulous even when her casting falters.
2. Walk through a training shift, choosing when to ask for relief and demonstrating recovery care without inventing new spell mechanics.
3. Discuss two useful apprenticeships with Mell and arrange the one she wants to try.

**Resolution:** Mell can continue supervised healing on shorter shifts or move toward hospice recordkeeping while retaining her training. Neither path is framed as quitting, and either leaves the main ward crews properly staffed.

**Reward and later consequence:** Mell makes Iona a stitched notebook cover and supplies a finite recovery bundle. During Veyr's evacuation she appears either beside a senior medic or at the reunification desk. Iona's optional ending scene shows her handing a task over before sitting down to eat.

## Soren's companion story

### SQ-S1 — The Price of Efficiency

**Available:** Chapter Two after the party identifies Soren's marks on the feeders. **NPC:** Anet, a former engineering colleague.

Anet has a public commendation praising a design she and Soren developed. She wants it destroyed. A survivor instead asks to preserve it beside evidence of what the design actually did.

1. Locate their service diagrams in a shuttered workshop and compare the harmless presentation drawing with the actual feeder connections.
2. Recover the commendation and a maintenance note without taking unrelated workers' wage records.
3. Meet Anet and the survivor together to agree on a truthful explanatory caption that credits authorship and records the harm.

**Resolution:** Place the original documents in Leth's archive with named authors, or display copies with the authors' names while protecting the survivor's private testimony. The historical evidence survives both routes; anonymous authorship is not offered as an escape from responsibility.

**Reward and later consequence:** Anet rebuilds Soren's worn casting focus as a modest equipment alternative. The epilogue archive contains a caption he helped write, including his signature. His optional dialogue no longer calls the feeder “an unfortunate application” of otherwise innocent work.

### SQ-S2 — Anyone Can Read It

**Available:** Chapter Three after SQ-S1. **NPCs:** Sen and Bryn, a carpenter maintaining a shelter.

Soren distributes an accurate repair sheet that nobody can follow without his training. Bryn has already repaired a loose brace using a sketch in the margin. Sen asks Soren to watch someone use his instructions before printing more.

1. Follow Bryn through a small repair on an inactive practice lantern, noting which instructions require unfamiliar tools or assumptions.
2. Adapt the sheet using common-tool diagrams and clearly marked checks. A local translator reviews its wording.
3. Hold a demonstration and ask a volunteer to repeat the procedure while Soren keeps his hands off the mechanism.

**Resolution:** Make the first demonstration a quiet workshop for maintainers or an open market-table lesson. Sen schedules the other format later; the choice determines whom Soren learns from first, not who receives essential knowledge.

**Reward and later consequence:** Bryn carves an open-hand emblem for Soren's focus and gives a finite equipment-repair payment. Later shelters display visibly corrected diagrams. In his epilogue workshop, another person leads the lesson while he sits with the students.

## Regional stories

### SQ-R1 — Keep a Light for Me

**Available:** Chapter One, inside Reedbank during preparations for the expedition. **NPCs:** Bessa and the child Rill.

Bessa has working emergency lanterns but several broken handles. Rill has painted little shelter signs and wants one carried out with the next group. This practical favor gives Reedbank faces before the party enters Merewatch.

1. Find two discarded straps at the occupied repair bench, within the camp's existing protection.
2. Fit them to the lantern handles and ask Bessa to check the repair. No extra magical charge is created.
3. Help Rill hang a painted sign at either the family meeting point or the shared meal table.

**Resolution:** Choose which gathering place receives the sign; Bessa marks the other with an ordinary chalk arrow. Nobody is left without directions. The task takes a few conversations and does not send the party outside the camp or impose a countdown.

**Reward and later consequence:** Bessa releases one potion from the expedition's optional supply allowance. The repaired lantern and chosen sign travel with the camp; the same sign appears in the final roadside shelter. If skipped, Bessa's helpers complete basic repairs without the personal decoration.

### SQ-R2 — A Wooden Bird

**Available:** Chapter Two after Orren reaches the refuge. **NPCs:** Orren and his daughter Elsi, an apprentice baker in Dunmere.

Orren asks who painted the bird on his coat. A maker's mark leads to Elsi. He wants contact but fears being required to perform a reunion he cannot feel.

1. Ask Orren what information he wants shared, then trace the bird through a stallholder's commission book.
2. Find Elsi and explain what Orren has permitted, without promising a cure or a familiar greeting.
3. Arrange their first contact at the refuge, following the boundaries each has requested.

**Resolution:** Help them exchange letters first or arrange a quiet meeting with Iona nearby, provided both consent. Neither route produces restored recognition. Elsi is allowed to be hurt; Orren is allowed to ask for another visit rather than a shared home.

**Reward and later consequence:** Orren carves a new bird as a party keepsake, while Elsi supplies a finite parcel of baked food. Their later scene shows them painting a bird together or exchanging drawings by post. Their relationship is developing, not resolved by a successful quest notification.

### SQ-R3 — The Last Graft

**Available:** Chapter Three after the mandatory orchard clearance. **NPC:** Jessa, the orchard keeper.

The main supply trees are secured, but a small terrace outside the cleared route holds Jessa's last graft from the orchard she planted with her late husband. Its survival is personal, not necessary to feed the evacuation.

1. Inspect the damaged tree and an old nursery plan with Jessa to find a viable cutting.
2. Reach the optional terrace and defeat a **Briar Widow**, a Hush growth with poison attacks, after learning its warning behavior from snapped thorn stems and venom marks.
3. Bring back the cutting and help prepare its new bed.

**Resolution:** Jessa can plant it beside the refuge kitchen or return it to a tended nursery plot. Both locations have ordinary protection and caretakers. No plant contains a recoverable human memory, and neither choice determines whether the refugees eat.

**Reward and later consequence:** Jessa provides a finite antitoxin bundle and a carved fruitwood token. The epilogue shows either a young tree beside Neri's kitchen or the nursery's first labeled sapling. It bears fruit only after time has passed.

### SQ-R4 — One More Performance

**Available:** Chapter Four while the city districts are being prepared to leave. **NPCs:** Pella, a theater manager, and her former stagehands.

The main evacuation already has shelter space assigned. Pella offers her little theater as an additional rest stop, but cannot decide whether to dismantle its stage for tables. The actors have scattered; the remaining crew want to give the neighborhood one final performance before departure.

1. Find the stagehands within the district and agree on a short show they can perform with their existing equipment.
2. Inspect the safe public rooms with a refuge organizer, choosing where supplies and resting benches can fit.
3. Help carry either the portable scenery or the dismantled stage benches to the allied refuge; witness a brief rehearsal rather than play a new minigame.

**Resolution:** Preserve a small stage for a traveling troupe or turn the boards into a communal dining table and keep the costumes for neighborhood storytelling. Both choices include the farewell show and respect the main evacuation schedule.

**Reward and later consequence:** Pella gives the party a distinctive stagecoat as an equipment appearance and a finite supply payment. Her epilogue becomes a touring performance at the shelters or a crowded supper where the old scenery decorates the walls.

## Carrying consequences into the campaign

Use dialogue, changed duties, returned objects, and short epilogue scenes to show outcomes. Do not assign a hidden morality score. The named choices above should be remembered individually, with one alternate scene or prop arrangement rather than separate campaign branches.

The default ending remains hopeful when quests are skipped: the main route establishes shelter, food, training, and records. Completed side stories let the player recognize particular people helping, the records they chose to share, and the new things they made together. The final roadside bell still reads **“Ring. Someone will come.”**
